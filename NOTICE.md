# Input attribution and terms

The MIT licence applies to original software and documentation,
including these acquisition tools. Agency inputs keep their own terms.

- **Rhode Island tabulation blocks.** Attribute the U.S. Census Bureau,
  2020 TIGER/Line tabulation-block shapefiles. The exact archive's
  tl_2020_44_tabblock20.shp.iso.xml permits use in products and publications
  with Census acknowledgement. Preserve its statistical-boundary, positional
  accuracy and trademark qualifications; the boundaries are not legal land
  descriptions. The original metadata remains inside the untouched archive.
- **County adjacency.** Attribute the U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 County Adjacency
  File. The [official product page](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/county-adjacency.2023.html)
  publishes the reference file. The [Census public-access policy](https://www2.census.gov/foia/ds_policies/ds027.pdf)
  describes the domestic copyright position for employee-created federal data.
  No contrary product restriction was found; no CC0 dedication is invented.
  Obtain the file directly from Census; it is not redistributed here.
- **Plant inventory.** Attribute the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency,
  eGRID2023 revision 2 (2025), Office of Atmospheric Protection, Clean Air and
  Power Division. The [eGRID-specific FAQ, question 25](https://www.epa.gov/egrid/frequent-questions-about-egrid)
  identifies the data as public domain and permits commercial and non-commercial
  use. The [EPA standard open-data licence](https://edg.epa.gov/EPA_Data_License.html)
  corroborates that basis. The [product page](https://www.epa.gov/egrid/detailed-data)
  identifies revision 2 and its release date. Its absence of a separate grant
  inside the workbook is therefore not an unresolved permission barrier.

Locally derived Census/plant/state CSVs preserve these sources' attribution.
Neither original nor prepared provider data are included in Git or release assets. Raw
comparative results are original computational outputs; they do not convert
agency inputs into MIT-licensed data. The complete input URLs, sizes and SHA256
values are in data/input-sources.json. No agency endorses this analysis.

Missing plant reports are not physical zeros; the studied quantities are assigned
point references, not exact residents, a complete emissions inventory or exposure.
Acquisition checks identify exact bytes. Neither current URLs nor checksums alone
preserve an input: retain the original bundle in durable storage as well.
